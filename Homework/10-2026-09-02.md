## 📝숙제 Homework

1.I gained as much as six pounds because I ate too much seafood. I had no idea abalone was that high in calories. So from now on I'm thinking of eating only vegetables.
🔁
해산물을 너무 많이 먹은 탓에 살이 6(육)파운드나 쪘어요. (✅) 
전복은 그렇게 칼로리가 높은지 몰랐어요. (✅) 
그래서 앞으로 야채만 먹을까 해요. (✅) 
💡 because of, due to (negative result)
 💡 even, rather
 💡 didn't know that, had no idea  
 💡 explain the reason (soft, nice tone)
 💡 Thinking about doing something; considering a plan

2.I'm relatively good at driving, but Busan is one of the hardest cities to drive in in Korea. Still, since I'm going there on a business trip anyway, I intend to drive myself — although I am worried I might get lost.
🔁 
보통 운전을 잘 하는 편인데 부산은 한국에서 운전하기 어려운 도시 중에 하나예요. (✅) 
그런데 부산에 출장하는 김에 직접 운전할 셈이기는 하지만 아마 길을 잃어버릴까 봐요. (❌) 
➡️그래도 부산에 출장하는 김에 직접 운전할 셈이에요. 아마
길을 잃어버릴까 걱정되’기는 하지만요.’ (✅) 
        =하지만 길을 잃어버릴까 걱정돼요 (✅) 
💡 to indicate that something has a certain tendency or is classified as such
 💡 while you're at it, on the occasion of + opportunity
 💡 intend to, plan to
 💡 Worried that something might happen
 💡 although/it's true that... but


3.Seeing that he drove almost 500 miles by himself, I guess he's tired. There's no point in even asking whether he'll drive again tomorrow. He'd better rest today.
🔁 
그는 500마일이나 직접 운전하는 걸 보니까 피곤한가 봐요. (❌) 
 ➡️ 오백마일이나 운전한 걸 보니까 피곤한가 봐요 (✅) 
그에게 내일 다시 운전할 거라고 물어보나 마나예요. (❌) 
➡️내일 다시 운전할지 물어보나 마나예요 (✅) 
오늘 쉬는 게 좋겠어요.  (✅) 
cf)그, 그녀, 그들 ➡️ We don’t use it for spoken language 
                         ➡️ Literary(poem, novel), only written language 
cf)운전할 거라고 ➡️ 운전할 거냐고 
💡 even, rather
 💡 judging from, seeing that
 💡 It seems...; I guess... (based on evidence, what you see)
 💡 Whether you do it or not, the result is the same)
 💡 It would be good to... / You'd better... 

4.. I had no idea he changed jobs. Seoul has a lot of opportunities whereas the rent is expensive, you know. So I'm not sure whether he'll be able to settle down there — I hope he does well.
🔁 
그는 회사를 옮긴 줄 몰랐어요.  (❌) 
➡️회사를 옮긴 줄 몰랐어요 (✅) 
서울은 취업 기회가 많은 반면에 월세가 꽤 비싸잖아요. (✅) 
그래서 거기에 정착할지 모르겠어요. (✅) 
새로운 회사에서 일을 잘하면 좋겠어요. (✅) 
 💡 didn't know that, had no idea
 💡 whereas, while, on the other hand
 💡 You know, As you know, remember
 💡 Not sure, the truth is unknown / uncertain information
 💡 I wish/hope 


5.. Is the traffic in Busan always this bad? There are more cars than I expected! It might rain later, so it would be good to leave early. I hope the traffic clears up a bit.
🔁 
부산은 길이 항상 이렇게 막힌가요? (❌) 
➡️부산은 길이 항상 이렇게 막히나요? (✅) 
     막히다 V + 나요 
생각보다 차가 너무 많은걸요! (✅) 
이따가 비가 올지도 모르니까 일찍 나가는 게 좋겠어요. (✅) 
교통이 곧 조금 풀리면 좋겠어요. (❌) 
 ➡️차가/교통이 안 막히면 좋겠어요 (✅) 
     =정체가 풀리면 좋겠어요(NEWS)(formal)
  cf)정체(traffic jam)가 풀리다 
💡 Wondering; asking politely; expressing curiosity
 💡 Expressing surprise, giving a gentle explanation, or emphasizing a fact  
💡 might/may/perhaps (not sure about a possibility)
 💡 It would be good to... / You'd better...
 💡 I wish/hope

## 📝숙제 Homework

1.I was going to turn off the lights, but I left them on because I was worried the movie might be scary.
🔁 불을 끄려다가 영화가 무서울까 봐서 불을 끄지 않았어요. (✅)
    =불을 끄려다가 영화가 무서울까 봐서 불을 켜 뒀어요. (✅)

  cf)켜다 + 아/어 두다 an expression used to indicate
that a certain act mentioned in the preceding statement is
completed and its result remains = 은/는 채로
(do something in advance, keep something ready, leave something open/on)

ex)소화제를 먹어 뒀어요.

ex)휴대폰을 충전했어요 vs 휴대폰을 충전해 뒀어요.
    🔁 I charged my phone vs I’ve charged my phone (so it’s ready when I need it)

ex)불을 켰어요 vs 불을 켜 뒀어요.
    🔁 I turned on the light vs I left the light on

ex)표를 샀어요 vs 표를 사 뒀어요

cf)준비하다 to prepare, ready

💡 Changed intention
💡 Worried that

2.Seeing that my friend keeps closing their eyes, they seem scared. It would be better to turn on the lights.
🔁 눈을 계속 감는 걸 보니까 무서운가 봐요. 불을 켜는 게 좋겠어요. (✅)
💡 Judging from
💡 Seems
💡 Advice

3.I forgot to set my alarm, so I woke up late. I should have gone to bed earlier.
🔁 알람을 맞추는 것을 잊어버린 탓에 아침 ‘일찍’ 일어났어요.
어젯밤에 일찍 잘걸 그랬어요. (❌)

➡️알람을 맞추는 것을 잊어버린 탓에 아침 ‘늦게’ 일어났어요.
어젯밤에 일찍 잘걸 그랬어요.(✅)
💡 Negative cause
💡 Regret

cf)알람을 맞추는 것을 (✅)

4.As I kept watching the movie, I came to like it. I found that it was more interesting than I expected.

🔁 그 영화를 보다 보니까 좋아해졌어요. (❌) 생각보다 재미있더라고요.
➡️ 그 영화를 보다 보니까 좋아하게 됐어요. (✅)
                                     =좋아졌어요 (✅)

cf)그 영화를 좋아해졌어요 (❌)

    -좋다하다(V) 좋다(A) + A+아/어지다
   (someone or something gradually becomes the specific state)

💡 Through continued activity
💡 Personal discovery

## 📝숙제 Homework

1.Since it’s the last weekend, it’ll probably be crowded, so it would be good for just us friends to go early in the morning. I heard they sell a lot of food there too.
🔁 마지막 주말인 만큼 사람이 많을 텐데 친구들끼리 아침 일찍 같이 가는 좋겠어요. 거기서 음식을 많이 판다던데요. (✅)
💡 since/given that
💡 I expect…, so/but…
💡 only that category being together
💡 It would be good to…
💡 I heard that…

2.I heard the Renaissance festival has a lot of music performances. But because the tickets were all sold out, I’m worried I won’t be able to go.
🔁 르네상스 축제는 공연이 많다던데요. 그런데 표가 다 팔린 탓에 못 갈까 봐 걱정이에요. (✅)
💡 I heard that… (background)
💡 because of, due to (negative result)
💡 Worried that something might happen

3.As soon as I arrived at the festival, it started to rain. Maybe because of the rain, there were only ten people.
🔁 축제에서 도착하자마자 비가 왔어요. 아마 비가 와서 그런지 사람이 10명밖에 없었어요. (✅)
💡 as soon as
💡 Maybe because…
💡 There is nothing but… / only

4.Judging from how my friend keeps looking at the food, he seems hungry. It would be good to buy two in advance.
🔁 음식을 계속 보는 걸 보니까 배고픈가 봐요.
     음식 2개를 사 두’는 게’ 좋겠어요. (✅)
💡 judging from, seeing that
💡 seems like, appears that
💡 do something in advance
💡 It would be good to…

5.Even if we go now, there are no tickets. They’ve all sold out, you know. There’s no point in asking.
🔁 지금 간들 표가 없어요. 표가 다 팔렸잖아요. 물어보나 마나 표가 없어요. (✅)
💡 Even if the action happens, the result will not change
💡 Remind the listener
💡 Doing it makes no difference

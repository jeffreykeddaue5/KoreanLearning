1.Because I got a wound, I couldn't go in the water. You can't go in with an open wound, you know. Parents naturally worry.
🔁

상처가 있는 채로 들어갈  수 없잖아요.
부모님들이 걱정하시는 법이에요. (✅) 

cf)상처 wound + 있다/없다/나다/깊다

    ex)마음의 상처(heartbroken) 

    ex)상처나다 to get hurt/to get a cut/wound 

💡 because of, due to, negative result 
💡 while doing, while being in a state

2.It looks like it's going to rain. Since the road seems like it'll be blocked, we'd better hurry. Even if we leave now, we might still be late, you know.
🔁 
비가 올 모양이에요. 
길이 막힐 걸 보니까 빨리 가는 게 좋겠어요.
지금 출발한들 아마 늦을 것 같은걸요.  (✅) 
                           ➡️늦을지도 모르는걸요 

cf)늦을지 몰라요 I don’t know whether.. 
    vs 늦을지도 몰라요 It might(uncertainty, maybe I’m not sure, 
                               (I don’t know the future) 
      vs 늦을 수도 있어요 It might(that is also possible, 
                               (you know future)

      -도 also, being late is also a possibility
    

cf)A/V+을/ㄹ지도 모르다 might/may/perhaps 
    (not sure about a possibility)

    ex)늦을지도 몰라요 
    ex)비가 올지도 몰라요 
    ex)조금 비쌀지도 몰라요 

💡 seems like, appears that, future
💡 It would be good to... / You'd better... 
💡 Even if...; expresses that something wouldn't change the outcome 
💡 Expressing surprise, giving a gentle explanation, or emphasizing a fact

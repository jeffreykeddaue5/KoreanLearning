## 📝숙제 Homework

1. I left the house with the lights on.
🔁불이 켠 채로 집에서 나갔어요. (❌) 
    ➡️(저는) 불을 켠 채로 집에서 나왔어요. (✅) 
💡 Use while being in a state 

cf)나가다 to go out, something or someone moving away from the speaker or reference point
 vs 나오다 to come out, something or someone moving toward the speaker or outside(I)  
2. I didn't know the movie would be so interesting.
🔁 영화가 너무 재미있는 줄 몰랐어요. (❌) 
     ➡️영화가 많이 재미있는 줄 몰랐어요 (✅) 
         =영화가 그렇게 재미있는 줄 몰랐어요 (✅) 
💡 Use didn't know 
cf)너무 too(negative), ex)너무 더워요, 불편해요 

3.He is quiet, whereas his younger brother is very talkative.
🔁 (그는) 조용하는 반면에  남동생은 이야기를 많이 해요. (❌) 
     (그는) 조용한 반면에 남동생은 이야기를 많이 해요 (✅) 
💡 Use whereas, whereas, while, on the other hand
      cf)조용하다 V? A? ➡️ A (✅) 
      cf)그는, 그녀는, 그들은 ➡️ Poem, novel....etc
          Instead of Using 그는, 그녀는, 그들은 We use 
          Person name, position 








4.Since I came to Korea, I also visited my friend, but I didn't know he would be so busy.
🔁 한국에 오는 김에 친구도 만나기는 하지만 너무 바쁜 줄 몰랐어요. (❌) 
    ➡️한국에 온 김에 친구도 만나기는 했지만 너무 바쁜 줄 몰랐어요. (✅) 
💡 Use while you're at it while you're at it, on the occasion of + opportunity
💡 Use although, although/it's true that... but
💡 Use didn't know, didn't know that, had no idea

5.Although he looks calm, I don't know what he's thinking.
🔁 (그는) 차분하기는 하지만 뭐 생각하는 지 모르겠어요. (✅) 
💡 Use although, although/it's true that... but
💡 Use not sure,not sure, the truth is unknown / uncertain information

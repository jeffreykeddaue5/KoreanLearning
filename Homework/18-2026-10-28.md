## 📝숙제 Homework

1.I was going to watch Breaking Bad without subtitles, but it was too difficult, so I watched it with subtitles. I’m thinking of watching it again next week.
🔁 자막 없이 브배를 보려다가 너무 어려워서 자막을 켠 채로 봤어요.
     다음 주에 브배를 다시 볼까 해요. (✅)
💡 Was going to…, but…
💡 Thinking about doing something

2.As I kept watching dramas, I ended up not being able to make my podcast. I should have done my work first.
🔁 드라마를 보다 보니까 팟캐스트를 못 만들게 됐어요.
     먼저 일을 할 ‘뻔했어요.’ (❌)
         ➡️먼저 일을 할’걸 그랬어요’ (✅)
💡 Through continued activity
💡 End up / come to
💡 I should have…

3.I heard this movie is as long as three hours. I’m worried I’ll have to go to the bathroom often. So I’m thinking of not drinking coffee before the movie.
🔁 이 영화가 3시간이나 ‘걸리는다던데요.’ (❌)
         ➡️이 영화가 3시간이나 ‘걸린다던데요’ (✅)
     화장실에 자주 갈까 봐 걱정이에요.
     그래서 영화 전에 커피를 안 마실까 해요.

cf)걸리다 + ㄴ/는다던데(요)

💡 I heard that…; reported info as background
💡 even, as many as
💡 Worried that something might happen
💡 Thinking about doing something

4.Since it’s my cheat day today, I’m going to eat a lot. Health food is good for your body, whereas it doesn’t taste that good. As I kept eating it every day, I found it a bit hard.
🔁 오늘은 치팅데이인 만큼 실컷 먹으려고 해요.
     건강식은 몸’으로’ 좋은 반면에 맛이 없는 편이에요. (❌)
         ➡️건강식은 몸’에’ 좋은 반면에 맛이 없는 편이에요. (✅)
     계속 먹다 보니까 은근히 어렵더라고요.

cf)몸’에 좋다/안 좋다’
    🔁 good for something

    에 ①place particle ②time particle
        ③for (the goal or objective of something)

    ex)몸에 좋다/안 좋다, 요리에 좋아요, 감기에 좋아요

💡 since/given that
💡 whereas / on the other hand
💡 As I kept doing…
💡 I found that…

5.If you watch the movie late at night, you’ll probably be tired tomorrow, so it would be better to watch it on the weekend. Whether you drink coffee or not, you’ll be tired anyway.
🔁 밤에 영화를 늦게 보면 내일 피곤할텐데 주말에 보는 게 좋겠어요.
     커피를 마시나 마나 피곤할 거예요. (✅)
💡 I expect…, so…
💡 It would be good to…
💡 Whether you do it or not

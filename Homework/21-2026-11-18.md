1. A: I heard you like cooking. Is that true? / B: Yes, I tend to cook every weekend. Last week I made gochujang stew, and I found it was spicier than I expected. / A: Oh, so you like spicy food.
🔁
A: 요리를 좋아한다던데요. 그렇죠? (✅) 
     =요리를 좋아’한다면서요’? (✅) 
B: 네, 주말마다 요리하는 편이에요. 지난 주 고추장찌개를 만들어 보니까 생각보다 맵더라고요!  (✅) 
A: 아 그럼… 매운 음식을 좋아하군요 (✅)

3.A: Is the mala tang a bit spicy, by any chance? / B: It’s slightly spicy, but it’s tasty. I also ordered lamb skewers in advance. / A: Oh, you prepared everything!
🔁 
A: 혹시 마라탕은 좀 매운가요?
B: 네, 약간 맵기는 하지만 꽤 맛있어요. 이미 양꼬치도 주문해 뒀어요.
A: 와! 다 준비했군요!  (✅) 

💡 Wondering; asking politely 
💡 although / it’s true that… but 
💡 do something in advance 
💡 Express realization 






4.I heard that restaurant’s mala tang is famous, but the line was long, so I almost couldn’t eat it. I should have gone a little earlier.
🔁 
그 식당은 마라탕이 유명하던데요. 
근데 줄이 너무 길어서 하마터면 못 먹을 뻔했어요. 
진작 먹으러 갈걸 그랬어요. (✅) 
 
💡 I heard that… 
💡 Something almost happened 
💡 I should have… 

5.The lamb skewers looked delicious, so I ordered as many as ten. But I found that my friend doesn’t like them. I had no idea.
🔁 
양꼬치가 ‘맛있는 걸 보니까’ 10개나 주문했어요.(❌) 
➡️양꼬치가 ‘맛있어 보이길래’ 10개나 주문했어요 (✅) 
근데 친구가 좋아하지 않더라고요.
그런 줄 몰랐어요.

cf)ㄴ/는 걸 보니까(you observe a fact or action) 
     “I saw something happening”
      vs 아/어 보이길래(you see an appearance/impression)
        “It looked(appearance) a certain way to me

cf)않더라고요 
    [안터라고요](ㅎ + ㄷ/ㄷ +ㅎ ➡️ ㅌ)
     
    ex)좋다[조타] 넣다[너타]

💡 even, rather 
💡 I found that…

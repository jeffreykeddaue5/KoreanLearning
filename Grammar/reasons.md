### 7. A/V-(으)ㄴ/는 탓에
**Form:** V-는 (present) · V-(으)ㄴ (past) · A-(으)ㄴ · N + 탓에
**Meaning:** because of…; due to… (with a negative result)

- 운동을 안 한 탓에 살이 많이 쪘어요.
  🔁 I gained a lot of weight because I didn't exercise.
- 버스를 놓친 탓에 수업에 늦었어요.
  🔁 I was late for class because I missed the bus. (L4)
- 앞사람이 모자를 쓴 탓에 화면이 잘 안 보였어요.
  🔁 I couldn't see the screen well because the person in front of me was wearing a hat. (L6)
- 날씨가 추운 탓에 감기에 걸렸어요.
  🔁 I caught a cold because it was so cold.
- 제 탓이에요.
  🔁 It's my fault.

❌ 날씨가 좋은 탓에 소풍을 갔어요. → ✅ 날씨가 좋아서 소풍을 갔어요. (탓에 needs a bad result)

✔ Usually followed by a negative result
✔ Puts blame on the cause; stronger than -때문에
✔ Similar: -는 바람에

### 16. A/V-거든요
**Meaning:** (because)…, you see; explains a reason in a soft, friendly tone

- 가: 민수 씨는 왜 학교에 안 왔어요?
  나: 민수 씨는 아프거든요.
  🔁 A: Why didn't Minsu come to school? B: He's sick, you see.
- 가: 여자친구랑 그 영화 봤어요?
  나: 그 영화는 여자친구 취향이 아니거든요. 그래서 같이 안 봤어요.
  🔁 A: Did you watch that movie with your girlfriend? B: It's not her taste, you see. So we didn't watch it together.
- 저 요즘 덥스텝을 듣거든요. 그런데 생각보다 재미있어요.
  🔁 I've been listening to dubstep lately, you see. And it's more fun than I expected.
- 가: 왜 이렇게 일찍 가요?
  나: 내일 아침에 시험이 있거든요.
  🔁 A: Why are you leaving so early? B: I have an exam tomorrow morning.
- 어제 잠을 못 잤어요. 옆집이 너무 시끄러웠거든요.
  🔁 I couldn't sleep last night. The neighbors were really loud, you see.

❌ (to a friend who was there) 어제 비가 왔거든요. → ✅ 어제 비가 왔잖아요. (they already know → -잖아요, #17)

✔ Gives a reason the listener didn't know
✔ Can also introduce background information before continuing a story (third example)
✔ Past: -았/었거든요

### 33. A/V-(으)니만큼
**Meaning:** since…; given that…

- 노력하니만큼 결과가 기대된다.
  🔁 Since I'm working hard, I'm looking forward to the result.
- 중요한 시험이니만큼 열심히 준비해야 해요.
  🔁 Since it's an important exam, you have to prepare hard.
- 처음 하는 일이니만큼 실수할 수도 있어요.
  🔁 Since it's your first time doing this, you might make mistakes.
- 비싼 돈을 냈으니만큼 제대로 배우고 싶어요.
  🔁 Since I paid a lot of money, I want to learn it properly.

✔ States a reason, often with an expectation that follows from it
✔ Formal; similar to -(으)ㄴ/는 만큼 in its "since/because" meaning
✔ Past: -았/었으니만큼 · Noun: N-(이)니만큼

### 36. A/V-길래
**Meaning:** since…; seeing that…; explaining what prompted an action

- 비가 오길래 택시를 탔어요.
  🔁 It was raining, so I took a taxi.
- 세일하길래 하나 더 샀어요.
  🔁 It was on sale, so I bought one more.
- 친구가 피곤해 보이길래 커피를 사 줬어요.
  🔁 My friend looked tired, so I bought them a coffee.
- 날씨가 좋길래 산책하러 나갔어요.
  🔁 The weather was nice, so I went out for a walk.
- 무슨 일이길래 사람이 이렇게 많아요?
  🔁 What's going on that there are so many people?

❌ 비가 오길래 우산을 가져가세요. → ✅ 비가 오니까 우산을 가져가세요. (no command or suggestion after -길래)
❌ 제가 배고프길래 밥을 먹었어요. → ✅ 배고파서 밥을 먹었어요. (-길래 is for something you noticed, not your own feelings)

✔ Introduces a situation that prompted an action
✔ Often followed by the speaker's response or decision
✔ Common when explaining past actions

### 38. A/V-아/어서 그런지
**Meaning:** maybe because…; perhaps because…

- 어제 잠을 못 자서 그런지 오늘 집중이 안 돼요.
  🔁 Maybe because I didn't sleep well last night, I can't concentrate today.
- 토요일이라서 그런지 길이 별로 안 막혔어요.
  🔁 Maybe because it was Saturday, the roads weren't very busy. (L14)
- 커피를 많이 마셔서 그런지 잠이 안 와요.
  🔁 Maybe because I drank a lot of coffee, I can't fall asleep.
- 날씨가 추워서 그런지 사람이 별로 없네요.
  🔁 Maybe because it's cold, there aren't many people.

❌ 피곤해서 그런지 일찍 자세요. → ✅ 피곤해 보이니까 일찍 자세요. (-아/어서 can't be followed by a command or suggestion, L7)

✔ Suggests a possible cause without being certain
✔ Common for feelings, physical conditions, and impressions
✔ Noun form: N-(이)라서 그런지

---

## Compare

| | Use it when… | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -아/어서 | plain reason (no commands after) | 비가 와서 늦었어요 |
| -(으)니까 | reason + command or suggestion is OK | 비가 오니까 우산 가져가세요 |
| -길래 (#36) | something you noticed made you act | 비가 오길래 택시를 탔어요 |
| -아/어서 그런지 (#38) | you're *guessing* the reason | 비가 와서 그런지 사람이 없어요 |
| -탓에 (#7) | blaming the cause of a bad result | 비가 온 탓에 경기가 취소됐어요 |
| -거든요 (#16) | telling the listener a reason they didn't know | 비가 오거든요 |
| -(으)니만큼 (#33) | given this fact, so naturally… (formal) | 비가 오니만큼 조심하세요 |

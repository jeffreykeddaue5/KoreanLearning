### 13. A/V-(으)면 좋겠어요
**Meaning:** I wish…; I hope…

- 내일 날씨가 좋았으면 좋겠어요.
  🔁 I hope the weather is nice tomorrow.
- 주말에 시간이 있으면 좋겠어요.
  🔁 I hope I have some time this weekend.
- 차가 안 막히면 좋겠어요.
  🔁 I hope there's no traffic. (L10)
- 다음에는 같이 갔으면 좋겠어요.
  🔁 I hope we can go together next time.
- 언젠가 자막 없이 보면 좋겠다고 생각했어요.
  🔁 I thought it would be nice to watch without subtitles someday. (L12)

❌ 교통이 곧 조금 풀리면 좋겠어요 → ✅ 차가/교통이 안 막히면 좋겠어요 (L10 — 정체가 풀리다 is news-style)
❌ (describing a past thought) …자막 없이 보면 좋겠어요 → ✅ …보면 좋겠다고 생각했어요 (L12)

✔ -았/었으면 좋겠어요 is very common and sounds a little stronger

### 19. V-(으)ㄹ까 하다
**Meaning:** I'm thinking about…; considering a plan

- 이번 주말에 영화를 볼까 해요.
  🔁 I'm thinking about watching a movie this weekend.
- 표가 없어서 다음 주에 갈까 해요.
  🔁 There were no tickets, so I'm thinking of going next week. (L6)
- 저녁 먹고 유튜브 볼까 해요.
  🔁 I'm thinking of watching YouTube after dinner. (L15)
- 머리를 자를까 해요.
  🔁 I'm thinking about getting a haircut.
- 타투를 할까 했는데 오래 걸릴까 봐 안 했어요.
  🔁 I was thinking of getting a tattoo, but I didn't because I was worried it'd take too long. (L7, + #20)

✔ The plan isn't firmly decided yet
✔ Your tutor's scale (L15): 볼까 해요 ≈ 50–60% sure · 볼 것 같아요 ≈ 80–90% sure
✔ Past: -(으)ㄹ까 했는데 = I was thinking of…, but

### 20. A/V-(으)ㄹ까 봐
**Meaning:** worried that something might happen; in case…

- 늦을까 봐 일찍 출발했어요.
  🔁 I left early because I was worried I'd be late.
- 비가 올까 봐 걱정이에요.
  🔁 I'm worried it might rain.
- 비가 올까 봐 우산을 가지고 왔어요.
  🔁 I brought an umbrella in case it rains.
- 가: 우산을 왜 가져왔어요?
  나: 비가 올까 봐요.
  🔁 A: Why did you bring an umbrella? B: In case it rains.
- 표가 없을까 봐 아침부터 기다렸거든요.
  🔁 I was worried there wouldn't be tickets, so I waited from the morning. (L6)
- 시험에 늦을까 봐 걱정이었어요.
  🔁 I was worried I'd be late for the exam. (L14)

❌ 오래 걸릴까 봐서 → ✅ 오래 걸릴까 봐 (no -서, L7)
✅ 길을 잃어버릴까 걱정돼요. = I'm worried I'll get lost. (L10 — 걱정되다 works directly after -(으)ㄹ까)

✔ The second clause is a worry or an action taken because of the worry
✔ Can end a sentence on its own: -(으)ㄹ까 봐요
✔ Don't mix up with #19: -(으)ㄹ까 **하다** = plan · -(으)ㄹ까 **봐** = worry

### 26. V-는 게 좋겠어요
**Meaning:** it would be good to…; you'd better…

- 일찍 출발하는 게 좋겠어요.
  🔁 It would be good to leave early.
- 오늘은 쉬는 게 좋겠어요.
  🔁 You'd better rest today. (L10)
- 비가 올 것 같으니까 우산을 가져가는 게 좋겠어요.
  🔁 It looks like rain, so you'd better take an umbrella.
- 밤에는 커피를 안 마시는 게 좋겠어요.
  🔁 It'd be better not to drink coffee at night.
- 알람을 두 개 맞추는 게 좋겠다고 생각했어요.
  🔁 I thought it would be good to set two alarms. (L14)

✔ Gives soft advice or a suggestion
✔ Negative: -지 않는 게 좋겠어요 / 안 V-는 게 좋겠어요
✔ The reason before it uses -(으)니까, not -아/어서 (L7)

### 40. V-(으)ㄹ걸 그랬다
**Meaning:** I should have…; regret about a past choice

- 아까 밥을 좀 먹을걸 그랬어요.
  🔁 I should have eaten something earlier.
- 시험 볼 때 너무 피곤해서 일찍 잘걸 그랬어요.
  🔁 I was so tired during the exam — I should have gone to bed early. (L14)
- 우산을 가져올걸 그랬어요.
  🔁 I should have brought an umbrella.
- 그 말을 하지 말걸 그랬어요.
  🔁 I shouldn't have said that.
- 괜히 샀어요. 싼 걸 살걸.
  🔁 I shouldn't have bought it. I should've just gotten the cheap one.

✔ Expresses regret about an action not taken
✔ Negative: -지 말걸 그랬다 = I shouldn't have…
✔ 그랬어요 can be dropped in casual speech

## Compare

| | Use it when… | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -(으)면 좋겠어요 (#13) | you wish or hope for something | 비가 안 오면 좋겠어요 |
| -는 게 좋겠어요 (#26) | you give advice | 우산을 가져가는 게 좋겠어요 |
| -(으)ㄹ까 하다 (#19) | you're considering a plan | 우산을 살까 해요 |
| -(으)ㄹ까 봐 (#20) | you're worried it might happen | 비가 올까 봐 우산을 샀어요 |
| -(으)ㄹ걸 그랬다 (#40) | you regret not doing it | 우산을 가져올걸 그랬어요 |

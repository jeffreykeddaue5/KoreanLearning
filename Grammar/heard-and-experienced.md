### 10. -(으)ㄴ/는/(으)ㄹ 줄 몰랐다
**Form:** V-(으)ㄴ/는/(으)ㄹ 줄 몰랐다 · A-(으)ㄴ 줄 몰랐다
**Meaning:** I didn't know that…; I had no idea…

- 공무원 시험에 합격할 줄 몰랐다.
  🔁 I didn't know I would pass the civil service exam.
- 영화가 그렇게 재미있는 줄 몰랐어요.
  🔁 I didn't know the movie would be that fun. (L2)
- 한국 배달 음식이 이렇게 빠른 줄 몰랐어요.
  🔁 I had no idea Korean food delivery was this fast. (L5)
- 우체국이 문을 닫은 줄 몰랐어요.
  🔁 I didn't know the post office was closed. (L1)
- 회사를 옮긴 줄 몰랐어요.
  🔁 I didn't know you'd changed jobs. (L10)

❌ 영화가 너무 재미있는 줄 몰랐어요 → ✅ 그렇게/많이 재미있는 줄 몰랐어요 (L2)
❌ 그는 회사를 옮긴 줄 몰랐어요 → ✅ 회사를 옮긴 줄 몰랐어요 (그/그녀/그들 aren't used in speech — use the name or drop it, L10)

✔ Expresses surprise at an unexpected fact or result
✔ Opposite: -(으)ㄹ 줄 알았다 (I knew/thought it would…) — 비가 올 줄 알았어요. 그래서 우산을 가져왔어요.

### 35*. A/V-더라고요
**Meaning:** I found that…; sharing something learned through personal experience

- 그 식당 가 봤는데 생각보다 괜찮더라고요.
  🔁 I tried that restaurant, and it was better than I expected.
- 어제 그 영화 봤는데 좀 무섭더라고요.
  🔁 I watched that movie yesterday, and it was kind of scary.
- 부산에 갔는데 길이 많이 막히더라고요.
  🔁 I went to Busan, and the traffic was really bad.
- 한국 사람들은 생각보다 일찍 출근하더라고요.
  🔁 I noticed Korean people go to work earlier than I thought.
- 얘기를 많이 하다 보니까 마음에 들더라고요.
  🔁 After talking a lot, I found I really liked them. (L14, with #37)

❌ 맛있다러고요 → ✅ 맛있더라고요 (stem + 더라고요 — from your practice sessions)
❌ 제가 어제 많이 먹더라고요. → ✅ 제가 어제 많이 먹었어요. (not for your own actions — it's for things you observed)

✔ Reports something personally observed or experienced
✔ Common when sharing experiences
✔ Unlike -대요, it doesn't simply report what someone else said

### 42. -다면서요?
**Form:** V-ㄴ/는다면서요? · A-다면서요? · N-(이)라면서요?
**Meaning:** I heard that… Is that true?

- 다음 달에 한국에 간다면서요?
  🔁 I heard you're going to Korea next month. Is that true?
- 이사했다면서요? 새 집은 어때요?
  🔁 I heard you moved. How's the new place?
- 민수 씨가 결혼한다면서요?
  🔁 I heard Minsu is getting married — is that right?
- 그 식당이 그렇게 맛있다면서요?
  🔁 I heard that restaurant is really good?
- 내일이 생일이라면서요?
  🔁 I heard tomorrow is your birthday?

✔ Asks the listener to confirm information heard earlier
✔ 가다 → 간다면서요?; 먹다 → 먹는다면서요?
✔ Past: -았/었다면서요?
✔ Noun: 학생이라면서요?

### 43. -다던데(요)
**Form:** V-ㄴ/는다던데(요) · A-다던데(요) · N-(이)라던데(요)
**Meaning:** I heard that…; introducing reported information as background

- 거기 주말에는 사람이 많다던데, 예약할까요?
  🔁 I heard that place gets crowded on weekends. Shall we make a reservation?
- 내일 비가 온다던데 우산 챙기세요.
  🔁 I heard it's going to rain tomorrow, so bring an umbrella.
- 그 영화 재미있다던데 같이 볼래요?
  🔁 I heard that movie is good — want to watch it together?
- 민수 씨가 요즘 바쁘다던데 다음에 만날까요?
  🔁 I heard Minsu is busy these days. Shall we meet another time?
- 거기가 유명한 맛집이라던데요.
  🔁 I heard it's a famous restaurant.

✔ Presents information heard from someone else
✔ Can lead into a question, suggestion, or contrasting comment
✔ Unlike -다면서요?, it doesn't ask for confirmation

### 50. V-아/어 보니(까)
**Form:** V-아/어 보니(까) · usually + -더라고요 or past tense
**Meaning:** when I actually tried / did it, I found that…

- 막상 가 보니까 볼거리가 많더라고요.
  🔁 Once I actually went, there was a lot to see.
- 확인해 보니까 다 품절됐더라고요.
  🔁 When I checked, everything was sold out.
- 먹어 보니까 생각보다 맛있었어요.
  🔁 When I tried it, it was tastier than I expected.

❌ 확인하다 보니 품절됐어요 → ✅ 확인해 보니까 품절됐더라고요 (one check that led to a discovery → -아/어 보니까; -다 보니 #37 is for doing something over and over)
❌ 확인하자마자 품절됐어요 → ✅ 확인해 보니까 품절됐더라고요 (-자마자 = B right after A, not "found out")

✔ The second half is what you found out, not something you decided
✔ 막상 + -아/어 보니까 = "once I actually did it" (it was different from what I expected)
✔ With 보다 (see/watch), just 보니까: 영화를 보니까 … (⚠ 봐 보니까 is on the To verify list)


### 57. V-았/었더니
**Form:** V-았/었더니 · usually about your own action
**Meaning:** I did A, and then (I found / the result was) B

- 창문을 열었더니 눈이 오고 있었어요.
  🔁 I opened the window, and it was snowing. (discovery)
- 아침에 커피를 두 잔 마셨더니 잠이 안 와요.
  🔁 I had two coffees this morning, and now I can't sleep. (result)
- 매일 운동했더니 몸이 가벼워졌어요.
  🔁 I worked out every day, and my body feels lighter. (result)
- 친구한테 전화했더니 안 받더라고요.
  🔁 I called my friend, but they didn't pick up.

❌ 제가 커피를 마시더니 잠이 안 와요 → ✅ 커피를 마셨더니 잠이 안 와요 (your own action → -았/었더니; -더니 without 았/었 is for what you saw someone else do)

✔ The second part is what happened or what you found, not a plan, command or suggestion
✔ Very close to -아/어 보니까 (#50) for discoveries, but -았/었더니 also covers results: 마셨더니 잠이 안 와요

## Compare

| | Where the info comes from | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -더라고요 (#35) | you saw or experienced it yourself | 그 식당 맛있더라고요 |
| -대요 | someone told you (plain report) | 그 식당 맛있대요 |
| -다면서요? (#42) | someone told you — you check with the listener | 그 식당 맛있다면서요? |
| -다던데(요) (#43) | someone told you — background for what you say next | 그 식당 맛있다던데 가 볼까요? |
| -줄 몰랐다 (#10) | you just found out and are surprised | 그 식당이 이렇게 맛있는 줄 몰랐어요 |
| -아/어 보니까 (#50) | you found out by trying or doing it once | 먹어 보니까 맛있더라고요 |
| -았/었더니 (#57) | you did A, and found B or B was the result | 먹었더니 배가 아파요 |
| -다 보니(까) (#37) | doing it over and over led to a change | 자주 먹다 보니까 좋아졌어요 |
| -(으)ㄴ/는 걸 보니(까) (#2) | you're guessing from evidence | 줄이 긴 걸 보니까 맛있나 봐요 |

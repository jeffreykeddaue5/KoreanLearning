# Conversation Endings

Sentence endings that add tone: surprise, shared knowledge, checking, wondering.

**Key:** V = verb · A = adjective · N = noun · 🔁 = translation · ✔ = note · ❌ → ✅ = common mistake · (L#) = from that lesson in `Homework/`

### 12. -는지 모르겠어요
**Form:** V-는지 (present) · A-(으)ㄴ지 · V/A-(으)ㄹ지 (future/guess) · -았/었는지 (past) + 모르겠어요
**Meaning:** I'm not sure (if/whether)…; I don't know…

- 지금 비가 오는지 모르겠어요.
  🔁 I don't know if it's raining right now.
- 내일 비가 올지 모르겠어요.
  🔁 I don't know if it will rain tomorrow.
- 그 카페가 언제 문을 여는지 모르겠어요.
  🔁 I don't know when that café opens. (L3)
- 오늘 볼 수 있는지 모르겠어요.
  🔁 I'm not sure if I can see it today. (L6)
- 서울에 정착할지 모르겠어요.
  🔁 I'm not sure if I'll settle down in Seoul. (L10)

❌ (whether it already opened) 가게가 문을 여는지 모르겠는데 → ✅ 가게가 문을 열었는지 모르겠는데 (L3)

✔ Expresses uncertain or unknown information
✔ Works with question words: 언제/어디/뭐/왜 + -는지 모르겠어요

### 17. A/V-잖아요
**Meaning:** you know…; as you know…; remember?

- 오늘은 일요일이잖아요.
  🔁 It's Sunday, you know.
- 우리 지난주에 만났잖아요.
  🔁 We met last week, remember?
- 오늘 주말이잖아요. 그래서 밤늦게 자도 돼요.
  🔁 It's the weekend, you know. So I can stay up late.
- 서울은 취업 기회가 많은 반면에 월세가 꽤 비싸잖아요.
  🔁 Seoul has lots of jobs, but the rent is pretty expensive, you know. (L10)
- 언어를 배우려면 단어를 많이 외워야 되잖아요.
  🔁 To learn a language you have to memorize a lot of words, you know. (L12)

✔ The speaker assumes the listener already knows the information
✔ Reminds the listener of shared knowledge
✔ Compare: -거든요 (#16) gives info the listener *doesn't* know

### 18. A/V-네요
**Meaning:** oh, …!; reacting to something you've just noticed

- 오늘 날씨가 좋네요.
  🔁 The weather is nice today!
- 블랙프라이데이라서 사람들이 많네요.
  🔁 There are lots of people because it's Black Friday!
- 이 식당 음식이 맛있네요.
  🔁 The food at this restaurant is delicious!
- 벌써 열 시네요.
  🔁 Oh, it's already ten o'clock.
- 한국어 진짜 잘하시네요.
  🔁 Your Korean is really good!

✔ The speaker is impressed by something they just learned from personal experience
✔ Noun: N-(이)네요
✔ Compare: -군요

### 22. A/V-군요
**Meaning:** oh, I see…; realizing or learning new information

- 한국에 사시는군요.
  🔁 Oh, you live in Korea.
- 아, 그래서 늦었군요.
  🔁 Oh, so that's why you were late.
- 한국 음식을 좋아하시는군요.
  🔁 So you like Korean food.
- 벌써 다 끝났군요.
  🔁 Oh, it's already all finished.

✔ Forms: V-는군요, A-군요, N-(이)군요 · Past: -았/었군요
✔ Often a reaction to what someone just told you; slightly more formal or written than -네요

### 24. A/V-지요 (-죠)
**Meaning:** …, right?; isn't it?

- 오늘 날씨가 좋지요?
  🔁 The weather is nice today, isn't it?
- 내일 회의 있죠?
  🔁 We have a meeting tomorrow, right?
- 그 영화 진짜 재미있었죠?
  🔁 That movie was really good, wasn't it?
- 커피 드실 거죠?
  🔁 You'll have coffee, right?
- 그럼요, 당연하죠.
  🔁 Of course!

✔ Seeks agreement or confirms something
✔ Also softens statements and makes rhetorical questions
✔ Usually shortened to -죠 in speech

### 27. A/V-는걸(요)
**Meaning:** (oh,) it's actually…; expressing surprise or a gentle explanation

- 생각보다 재미있는걸요.
  🔁 It's more interesting than I expected.
- 생각보다 차가 너무 많은걸요!
  🔁 There are way more cars than I thought! (L10)
- 가: 많이 기다렸어요?
  나: 아니요, 저도 방금 왔는걸요.
  🔁 A: Did you wait long? B: No, I just got here myself.
- 가: 한국어 잘하시네요.
  나: 아직 멀었는걸요.
  🔁 A: Your Korean is good. B: Oh, I still have a long way to go.

✔ Forms: V-는걸요, A-(으)ㄴ걸요 · Past: -았/었는걸요
✔ Can also gently disagree or emphasize a fact

### 30. A/V-(으)ㄴ가(요)? / V-나요?
**Meaning:** I wonder…; asking politely or with curiosity

- 지금 출발한 건가요?
  🔁 Have they left already?
- 부산은 길이 항상 이렇게 막히나요?
  🔁 Is traffic in Busan always this bad? (L10)
- 여기서 사진 찍어도 되나요?
  🔁 Is it okay to take pictures here?
- 이거 얼마인가요?
  🔁 How much is this?
- 그 식당 아직 문 열었나요?
  🔁 Is that restaurant open yet?

❌ 막힌가요? → ✅ 막히나요? (막히다 is a verb → -나요; -(으)ㄴ가요 is for adjectives, L10)

✔ Forms: V-나요?, A-(으)ㄴ가요?, N-인가요? · Past: -았/었나요?
✔ Softer and more polite than a direct question

## Compare

| | Use it when… | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -네요 (#18) | reacting to what you notice right now | 사람이 많네요! |
| -군요 (#22) | "oh, I see" — learning something, often from what you're told | 사람이 많군요 |
| -더라고요 (#35) | telling someone what you noticed earlier | 사람이 많더라고요 |
| -잖아요 (#17) | the listener already knows | 주말이라 사람이 많잖아요 |
| -거든요 (#16) | the listener doesn't know — you explain | 오늘 세일하거든요 |
| -지요/-죠 (#24) | checking they agree | 사람 많죠? |
| -나요?/-(으)ㄴ가요? (#30) | soft, polite question | 사람이 많나요? |
| -는걸요 (#27) | "actually…" — mild surprise or pushback | 생각보다 사람이 많은걸요 |

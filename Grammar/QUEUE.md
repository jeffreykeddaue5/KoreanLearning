# Grammar Queue

Points to learn next, 2 a week. Grammar from the Wednesday lesson always comes first; lesson-intake fills the rest of the week from the top of this list, then deletes the line once the point gets a number in `Grammar/`.

Order: everyday conversation → common in media → TOPIK. Reorder or veto freely. A line can say what it pairs with.

## 1. Everyday conversation

- -기로 하다 — decide to; agree to
- -(으)ㄹ 만하다 — worth doing; good enough (볼만하다, 먹을 만하다)
- -는데도 — even though · vs #48 N(이)어도, -아/어도
- -던데(요) — I noticed… (soft, leaves room to reply) · vs #35 -더라고요
- -대요 / -래요 / -재요 / -냬요 — short reported speech (they said…) · vs #42, #43
- -(으)ㄹ수록 — the more…, the more…
- -는 대로 — as soon as; just as (집에 도착하는 대로, 말한 대로)
- -아/어야지(요) — I should / I'm going to (resolution)
- -(으)ㄹ 겸 — also for the purpose of (운동도 할 겸)
- -(으)ㄴ/는 척하다 — pretend to
- -기는요 — modest denial (잘하기는요)
- -(으)ㄹ 수밖에 없다 — have no choice but to
- -곤 하다 — used to; often do
- -(으)ㄴ/는 데다(가) — on top of, besides

## 2. Common in media (dramas, YouTube)

- -더니 — (I saw) A, and then B / A changed into B
- -다니(요)! — I can't believe…
- -다가는 — if you keep doing that, (something bad will happen)
- -아/어 봤자 — even if you do, it's no use
- -고 말다 — end up (finally / against your will)
- -(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 — there's no way…
- -는다니까(요) — I'm telling you; I said so
- -(으)ㄹ 줄 알았다 — I knew it; I thought… · vs #10 -줄 몰랐다
- -(으)ㄹ락 말락 하다 — almost, on the verge

## 3. TOPIK (intermediate)

- -기 마련이다 — naturally / bound to
- -(으)ㄹ 뿐만 아니라 — not only… but also
- N에 따라(서) — depending on
- -(으)ㅁ에도 불구하고 — despite
- -는 한 — as long as
- -(으)ㄴ/는 데 반해 — whereas · vs #5 -반면에

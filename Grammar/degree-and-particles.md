### 4. -(으)ㄴ/는 만큼
**Form:** V-(으)ㄴ/는 만큼 · A-(으)ㄴ 만큼
**Meaning:** as much as…; to the extent that…; since…

- 웃는 만큼 인생은 행복해진다.
  🔁 The more you smile, the happier life becomes.
- 공부하는 만큼 한국어 실력이 늘 거예요.
  🔁 Your Korean will improve as much as you study. (L1)
- 먹고 싶은 만큼 드세요.
  🔁 Eat as much as you want.
- 기대한 만큼 재미있었어요.
  🔁 It was as fun as I expected.
- 목표가 많은 만큼 어려운 점도 있어요.
  🔁 Since I have a lot of goals, there are some hard parts too. (L12)

✔ Expresses a proportional relationship
✔ Can also mean "since/because" (see #33 -(으)니만큼)
✔ Noun: N만큼 = as much as N (형만큼 키가 커요)

### 6. -는 법이다
**Form:** V-는 법이다 · A-(으)ㄴ 법이다
**Meaning:** it's natural that…; things tend to…

- 계속 노력하면 실력이 느는 법이다.
  🔁 If you keep trying, you naturally improve.
- 처음에는 누구나 실수하는 법이에요.
  🔁 Everyone makes mistakes at first.
- 버스를 놓친 탓에 늦게 왔지만 그런 일은 가끔 있는 법이에요.
  🔁 I was late because I missed the bus, but those things happen sometimes. (L4)
- 목표가 많은 만큼 어려운 점이 있는 법이에요.
  🔁 When you have a lot of goals, there are naturally some hard parts. (L12)

❌ 늦게 와서 가끔 그런 법이에요 → ✅ 늦게 왔지만 그런 일은 가끔 있는 법이에요 (L4)

✔ States a general truth or principle
✔ Often used in advice
✔ Similar: -기 마련이다

### 9. -(으)ㄴ/는/(으)ㄹ 셈이다
**Meaning:**
- V-(으)ㄹ 셈이다 — to intend to; to plan to
- V-(으)ㄴ/는 셈이다 — it amounts to…; it's practically…

- 어떻게 할 셈이야?
  🔁 What are you planning to do?
- 거의 다 끝난 셈이에요.
  🔁 It's practically done.
- 외출하는 김에 우산을 가져갈 셈이에요.
  🔁 Since I'm going out anyway, I plan to take an umbrella. (L3)
- 이렇게 비싼 줄 몰랐지만 하나 살 셈이에요.
  🔁 I didn't know it was this expensive, but I plan to buy one. (L5)
- 매일 30분씩 공부하니까 일주일에 세 시간 반 공부하는 셈이에요.
  🔁 I study 30 minutes a day, so it comes to three and a half hours a week.
- 이 가격이면 거의 공짜인 셈이에요.
  🔁 At this price, it's basically free.

❌ (a plan) 확인하는 셈이에요 → ✅ 확인할 셈이에요 (intention uses -(으)ㄹ, L3)

✔ The intention meaning is common in conversation, often when questioning someone's plan
✔ Similar (intention): -(으)려고 하다
✔ ⚠ Whether 셈이다 sounds natural for *your own* plan is on the To verify list in `KoreanProgress.md`

### 14. -는 편이다
**Form:** V-는 편이다 · A-(으)ㄴ 편이다
**Meaning:** tend to…; rather; on the … side

- 저는 매운 음식을 잘 먹는 편이에요.
  🔁 I'm pretty good with spicy food.
- 이 식당은 가격이 싼 편이에요.
  🔁 This restaurant is on the cheap side.
- 저는 커피를 많이 마시는 편이에요.
  🔁 I tend to drink a lot of coffee. (L13)
- 집에서 대학교까지 교통이 보통 복잡한 편이었어요.
  🔁 Traffic from home to campus was usually on the heavy side. (L14)
- 저는 일찍 자는 편이 아니에요.
  🔁 I'm not really someone who goes to bed early.

✔ Describes a tendency or general classification without stating it absolutely
✔ Negative: -는 편이 아니다

### 15. N끼리
**Meaning:** among themselves; only with (the same group)

- 친구들끼리 여행을 갔어요.
  🔁 We went on a trip, just us friends.
- 우리끼리 얘기해요.
  🔁 Let's keep this between us.
- 친한 사람들끼리는 반말을 써요.
  🔁 People who are close use casual speech with each other.
- 주말에 가족끼리 저녁을 먹었어요.
  🔁 We had dinner, just the family, on the weekend.

✔ Only people or things in the same group, together

### 21. N(이)나
**Meaning:**
1. …or something; just (lowering expectations)
2. as many as…; a whole… (more than expected)

- 밥이나 먹으러 갈까요?
  🔁 Shall we just go grab a bite or something?
- 커피나 한잔할까요?
  🔁 Want to grab a coffee or something?
- 쇼핑하는 데 열 시간이나 걸렸어요.
  🔁 Shopping took a whole ten hours.
- 맛집이라서 한 시간이나 줄을 섰어요.
  🔁 It's a popular place, so we waited in line for a whole hour.
- 오백 마일이나 운전했어요.
  🔁 He drove a whole 500 miles. (L10)

✔ Meaning 2 attaches to numbers and amounts
✔ Also plain "or": 커피나 차 = coffee or tea

### 23. N밖에 없다
**Meaning:** there is nothing but…; only…

- 물밖에 없어요.
  🔁 There's nothing but water.
- 남은 자리가 맨 앞자리밖에 없었어요.
  🔁 The only seats left were in the very front row. (L6)
- 저는 한국어를 1년밖에 안 배웠어요.
  🔁 I've only studied Korean for a year.
- 오늘은 만 원밖에 없어요.
  🔁 I only have 10,000 won today.
- 기다릴 수밖에 없어요.
  🔁 There's nothing to do but wait.

❌ 맨 앞자리밖에 없었거든요 → ✅ 남은 자리가 맨 앞자리밖에 없었거든요 (say what you're talking about — the *remaining* seats, L6)
❌ 1년밖에 배웠어요 → ✅ 1년밖에 안 배웠어요 (밖에 needs a negative)

✔ 밖에 is always followed by a negative (없다, 안, 못, 모르다)
✔ -(으)ㄹ 수밖에 없다 = have no choice but to

### 34. N(이)라도
**Meaning:** at least…; …if nothing else

- 시간이 없으면 커피라도 마셔요.
  🔁 If you don't have time, at least have a coffee.
- 바쁘면 문자라도 보내 주세요.
  🔁 If you're busy, at least send me a text.
- 주말에 산책이라도 할까요?
  🔁 Shall we at least go for a walk this weekend?
- 늦었지만 지금이라도 시작해요.
  🔁 It's late, but let's at least start now.

✔ Not the best option, but the most acceptable one available

## Compare

| | Use it when… | Example |
|---|---|---|
| N밖에 + negative (#23) | "only" — less than you'd like | 만 원밖에 없어요 |
| N만 | "only" — neutral | 만 원만 있어요 |
| N(이)나 (#21) | "a whole…" — more than expected | 만 원이나 썼어요 |
| N(이)나 (#21) | "…or something" — casual suggestion | 커피나 마셔요 |
| N(이)라도 (#34) | "at least" — not ideal but OK | 커피라도 마셔요 |
| -는 편이다 (#14) | a tendency, said softly | 많이 먹는 편이에요 |
| -는 법이다 (#6) | a general truth | 처음엔 다 어려운 법이에요 |
| -(으)ㄴ/는 만큼 (#4) | proportion — as much as | 노력한 만큼 늘어요 |
| -(으)ㄴ 셈이다 (#9) | "it basically amounts to" | 다 끝난 셈이에요 |

## Lesson mistakes & hard words
- ✔ 운전하는 → 운전한 걸 보니까 (L10): 걸어온 걸 보니까
- ✔ 파는 → 팔리는 걸 보니까 (L6): 마스크가 빨리 팔리는 걸 보니까
- ✔ 막상 (unprompted, twice) → done ✓
- ✔ 은근히 (with hint): 은근히 재미있더라고요
- ✏️ 어쩐지 (with hint): 어쩐지 이 행사는 3달러더라고요 → 어쩐지 입장료가 싸더라고요

## Grammar notes
- **-아/어 보니까** = "when I actually did X, I found Y" — discovery, not "because". One-time action. Pairs with -더라고요.
- **-다 보니까** (#37) = kept doing X over and over → result.
- **-길래** = "I noticed X, so I did Y". No command after it; not your own feelings; others' feelings → -아/어 보이길래.
- **-나 보다** = guess from evidence you have now; the guess can be about now or the past, not the future (→ -(으)ㄹ 것 같다).
- Already-finished state → 분장했길래 (they'd already dressed up).
- ⚠ 봐 보니까 → just 보니까 / 막상 보니까 (worth checking)
